**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **STAR WARS Zero Company™**. This trainer provides optional advantages for tactical missions and base operations, including enhanced survivability, unlimited action points, Advantage points, and expanded access to campaign resources.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Operators receive three action points per turn. Movement consumes AP based on distance, and available destinations are shown on the battlefield. Use the trainer's unlimited AP option to reposition freely, reach cover, or combine movement with attacks and abilities.

### Tools & Inventory

Hawks and squad members can be customized through appearances, combat specializations, loadouts, gear slots, and abilities. Configure the squad from the Den before deployment, then use the trainer to preserve resources while testing different equipment and operator combinations.

### World & Environment

The Den is Zero Company's base of operations and contains the Holotable, where tactical missions and non-combat operations are selected for each cycle. Operations consume Intel and can reward items, credits, influence, and relationship benefits. Complete conversations and activities before deploying, since missions advance the campaign cycle.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is turn-based and uses health points, action points, cover, ranged and melee attacks, backup attacks, overwatch, rally actions, and Advantage-based special abilities. The trainer can protect operators, prevent injuries, and provide unlimited AP or Advantage for more experimental strategies. Permadeath is optional in the base game, but injury protection is useful when enabled.
